hey there 👋

Bridging the gap between security and research.

The school firewall keeps us safe — but sometimes it gets in the way of homework. The Privacy Panel is the friendly, paper-trailed way to ask for a site to be unblocked, with your teacher's blessing.

How it works

three steps, that's it
1.
You fill in a form

Tell us the link, your subject, and why you need it.

2.
Your teacher signs off

They get CC'd on the email and reply with the magic word: "approved".

3.
IT reviews & publishes

Approved sites are added to the public list so future you doesn't have to ask twice.
